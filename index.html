Each RINGO ring begins as raw zirconia powder — the same material used in aerospace engineering and dental-grade implants. It is hydraulically pressed under enormous pressure into a near-net-shape ring blank, then sintered in high-temperature kilns at over 1,400°C for a minimum of 12 hours.

The result is a monolithic ceramic body with a crystalline microstructure that is scratch-resistant, hypoallergenic, and dimensionally stable for life. The NFC antenna and chip are then encapsulated within the body during the pressing process, making them truly integral to the ring — not adhered, not coated, not retrofitted.

A final multi-stage lapping and polishing process achieves the smooth, jewellery-grade surface finish RINGO is known for. No coating. No plating. The colour is intrinsic to the ceramic itself.

Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How does RINGO process payments?
RINGO uses passive NFC technology compliant with ISO/IEC 14443. Once linked to your bank card or digital wallet, it communicates with contactless terminals using the same encrypted tokenisation as your physical contactless card — your actual card number is never exposed.
Does it need charging?
No — never. RINGO is completely battery-free. The NFC chip draws its tiny operating power from the electromagnetic field emitted by the payment terminal at the moment of transaction. There is nothing to charge, nothing to replace, and nothing to run flat at the wrong moment.
Which banks and wallets are compatible?
RINGO is compatible with all major UK banks, Visa and Mastercard cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and most digital wallets. The setup process walks you through linking your preferred payment method securely via our app.
Is there a spending limit?
Contactless transactions in the UK currently have a limit of £100 per transaction without PIN verification. This applies to RINGO in the same way it applies to your contactless card. For transactions above £100, some terminal setups may request additional verification.
How do I find my correct ring size?
Every RINGO order includes a complimentary ring sizer tool in the box. We also provide a printable sizing guide on our size page. We recommend wearing your sizer for a full day before confirming, as finger size can vary with temperature and time of day. We offer one free size exchange within 30 days.
Can I wear it in the shower or swimming?
Yes. RINGO is rated IP68 — independently tested to withstand submersion to 1.5 metres for up to 30 minutes. It is safe to wear while showering, swimming in pools, and in the sea. The ceramic shell is fully sealed and chemically inert.
What if my ring is lost or stolen?
You can instantly freeze your RINGO from the app, or by contacting your linked bank directly — exactly as you would freeze a lost contactless card. The ring itself contains no personal data; it simply carries an encrypted token that can be revoked remotely.
Why only 100 rings in the Founding Edition?
The Founding Edition is a deliberate limited run — it allows us to gather real-world feedback from a select group of early adopters, ensure every ring meets our quality standards, and honour those who believe in RINGO from day one with founding member pricing and recognition.
